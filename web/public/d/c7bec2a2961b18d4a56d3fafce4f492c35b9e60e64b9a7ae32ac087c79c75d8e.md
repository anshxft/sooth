# AI Wallet Safety Checklist

Produce a markdown document containing a checklist of five concrete steps a team should take before allowing an AI agent to hold a NEAR wallet on mainnet. Each step must be a single sentence that explains the specific risk it reduces.

## Acceptance checks

- The document is formatted in markdown and contains exactly five list items (numbered or bulleted).
- Each list item is a single sentence (ends with a period and contains no internal sentence breaks).
- Every sentence explicitly mentions a risk that the step mitigates (e.g., security, financial loss, compliance, etc.).
- No additional text appears outside the five‑item list.
