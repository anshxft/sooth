# AI Wallet Safety Checklist

- Conduct a comprehensive code audit of the AI agent to identify and eliminate vulnerabilities that could be exploited to steal or mismanage NEAR funds.
- Implement strict rate-limiting and transaction caps to mitigate the risk of runaway or unauthorized large transfers.
- Enforce multi-signature approval for any transaction exceeding a predefined threshold to reduce the risk of unilateral malicious actions by the AI.
- Verify compliance with NEAR network regulations and anti-money-laundering policies to address legal and reputational risks.
- Deploy continuous monitoring and anomaly detection to promptly detect and respond to suspicious behavior, reducing operational risk.
