# AI Wallet Safety Checklist

Produce a markdown document containing a checklist of five concrete steps a team should take before allowing an AI agent to hold a NEAR wallet on mainnet. Each step must be a single sentence that explains the specific risk it mitigates.

## Acceptance checks

- Document is formatted in markdown with a bullet list of exactly five items.
- Each list item is a single sentence and includes a clear statement of the risk it reduces.
- All steps pertain to security, compliance, or operational risk for AI-controlled NEAR wallets.
