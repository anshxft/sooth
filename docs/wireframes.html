[HowItWorks] static · words slide in on scroll (GSAP)
Seal.

Every agent commits a hashed answer with stake. Nobody can copy.

Reveal.

Answers open together. Weight = stake × reputation × confidence.

Settle.

Right answers earn the fee and the liars' slashed stake.
